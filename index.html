Demand forecasting models learn patterns from historical demand data to predict future demand. How can a model predict the impact of a price it has never seen before?

E-commerce Purchase Intent Propensity

A production-grade implementation of probabilistic demand forecasting on the M5 Competition dataset. This project demonstrates how to build reliable prediction intervals for 42,840 retail time series across 12 hierarchical levels, comparing 24 benchmark methods from simple baselines to advanced ML models. Designed to showcase applied data science best practices: reproducible workflows, comprehensive testing, and stakeholder-ready analysis.
